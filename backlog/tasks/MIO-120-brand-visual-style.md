## Goal

A brand and visual style for MIO Games: logo usage, colour palette, typography, tone, and two moods
(bright and playful for the player widget, calm and trustworthy for the consoles).

## Scope

- Style guide in `design/brand/`.
- Design tokens updated from the style guide (MIO-122).

## Acceptance criteria (when unblocked)

- Style guide approved by the owner.
- Tokens regenerated and every Storybook story renders with the new style without code changes in screens.

## Notes

Until materials exist, the prototype and Storybook use a neutral placeholder theme.
